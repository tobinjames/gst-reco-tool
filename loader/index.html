ITC is often booked outside the Purchase Register: expenses through journals, small bills through payment vouchers. The Purchase Register is the main file; step 3 shows whether anything sits outside it.

1 · Purchase register, columnar ITC reco · Annual

  1. Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Account Books → Purchase Register.
  2. Set the period with Alt+F2 (the month or the whole year). Open the month list with Enter if needed.
  3. Switch to the Columnar view (Alt+F5, or the Columnar button on the right).
  4. In the columnar options turn on Supplier Invoice No., Supplier Invoice Date, GSTIN/UIN and Gross Total, and keep all ledger columns.
  5. Export (Ctrl+E; Alt+E in older releases) → file format Excel (Spreadsheet) → Send.
  6. Drop the .xlsx here. The Loader picks the purchase / expense ledgers for taxable value and the Input CGST / SGST / IGST / Cess ledgers for tax. Check the chips under step 2b.

Option names vary slightly between TallyPrime releases: if one is missing, look under F12 Configure.

2 · Journals and payment vouchers with GST ITC reco · Annual

  • Don't use the columnar Journal or Payment Register: it gets one column per ledger and becomes unmanageable.
  • They all pass through the Input CGST / SGST / IGST / Cess ledgers. For now, open those ledgers (step 3 below) to list them and review them alongside the reconciliation. Loading the GST ledgers directly, in detailed format, is being built.

3 · Check nothing is missing ITC reco · Annual

  1. Display More Reports → Account Books → Ledger → open Input CGST (then SGST, IGST, Cess), same period.
  2. The debit total for the period should equal the CGST (SGST, IGST, Cess) total of all registers loaded here (shown in the preview under step 2b).
  3. A difference is ITC booked through journals, payments or other voucher types that are not in the files loaded. Open the ledger with Alt+F1 to see the voucher types.

Debit notes / purchase returns

  • Don't load the Debit Note Register as a purchase register yet: the Loader reads every row as an invoice, so returns would be added instead of deducted.
  • Suppliers' credit notes appear on the 2B side (CDNR) and show up in the reconciliation as "in 2B, not in books" for review.

Sales register Annual · Revenue

  1. Display More Reports → Account Books → Sales Register.
  2. Same steps: period, Columnar, turn on Voucher No., GSTIN/UIN and Gross Total, then Export to Excel.
  3. Credit notes: export the Credit Note Register the same way and map its type column (or the Loader reads negative amounts as credit notes).

Zoho keeps ITC in three places: Bills, Expenses and Manual Journals. Export each one that carries GST and drop them here together.

Bills (purchases) ITC reco · Annual

  1. Purchases → Bills.
  2. Open the menu (☰) at the top right → Export Bills.
  3. Choose the date range, format XLSX (or CSV) → Export.

Expenses ITC reco · Annual

  1. Purchases → Expenses → menu (☰) → Export Expenses.
  2. Date range, XLSX → Export.
  3. In step 2b map Supplier invoice no. to the reference / invoice# column and check the taxable and tax columns. Expenses without GST are not compared.

Manual journals ITC reco · Annual

  1. Accountant → Manual Journals → menu (☰) → Export.
  2. Needed only if ITC is booked by journal (for example reverse charge or ITC reversals). The export has one row per journal line, so map the input-tax account lines carefully, or keep these for manual review.

Invoices (sales) Annual · Revenue

  1. Sales → Invoices → menu (☰) → Export Invoices, date range, XLSX → Export.
  2. For credit notes: Sales → Credit Notes → Export.

Check nothing is missing

  • Reports → Account Transactions (or the General Ledger) for the Input CGST / SGST / IGST accounts: the period's debits should equal the tax totals of everything loaded here.
  • Vendor credits (purchase returns) are not read as returns yet, so don't load them as purchases.

Good to know

  • Zoho exports one row per item line. That is fine: lines are added up to invoice level.
  • Columns such as Bill Number, GST Identification Number (GSTIN), Item Total, CGST, SGST, IGST map automatically.

Any accounting software or Excel

  • Export the GST purchase / sales register (for Busy, Marg and others, the GSTR-2 / GSTR-1 register) to .xlsx or .csv.
  • One row per invoice or per item line. Needed columns: invoice date, invoice no. (the supplier's number for purchases), taxable value. Strongly recommended: GSTIN, CGST, SGST, IGST, Cess.
  • Title rows above the header and a total row at the bottom are fine; the Loader finds the header row itself and skips totals.
  • Column names don't matter: map them under step 2b. Mappings are remembered for registers with the same columns.

Menus as of November 2026. The GST portal, Tally and Zoho change button names from time to time: if one differs, look for the nearest Download or Export.
